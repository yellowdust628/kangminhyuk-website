Recruitment privacy notice

Effective November 12, 2026. Information about international processing was updated on November 12, 2026.
Operator: MIN HYUK KANG (강민혁)
Privacy contact: druminghyuk@gmail.com

This notice covers applications for and administration of the first DrumUp closed test linked from the website. The app privacy policy explains how the app processes audio and practice history on your device.

1. What we collect and why

Required information
The email address of the Google Account you use for Google Play, your device model and Android version, your confirmations of eligibility and age, your consent, and the time of submission.
Optional information
Your drum practice experience. You can apply without answering this question.
Purposes
Selecting and contacting testers, enrolling selected testers in Google Play, identifying issues across device environments, and managing feedback.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, or date of birth, or use application information for advertising or promotional mailing lists. If you email feedback, the app privacy policy describes how we handle your email address, any display name, and message contents.

2. Retention and deletion

  • If you are not selected, we delete your information within 30 days after recruitment ends.
  • If you are selected, we delete your information within 30 days after testing ends.
  • If we no longer need the information or you withdraw your application, we delete unnecessary information without undue delay.

We delete application responses and any downloaded copies of the list. We also remove Google Play tester registrations that are no longer needed. If the law requires retention, we keep only the necessary information for the required period.

3. Google services and international processing

We use Google Forms, Gmail, and Google Play Console for recruitment. The operator manages responses in a private account and does not share responses or email lists with other applicants.

Service provider
Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, California 94043, USA. Google LLC is the provider under the Google Terms of Service applicable in South Korea. Contact Google through its privacy contact form or contact the operator about your application.
Google Forms
We receive, store, and review applications. Submitting the form sends your email address, device and Android version, eligibility and age confirmations, consent, submission time, and any optional drum experience answer to Google over the internet.
Gmail
We use email to send selection and participation instructions and answer questions. Sending or receiving email transmits and processes the sender and recipient addresses, any display name, subject, message, and send or receipt time over the internet. We do not include complete applications or other applicants’ addresses in these messages.
Google Play Console
After selection, the operator sends only the selected testers’ Google Account email addresses over the internet when adding or updating the tester list. We use the list to set test access. Device details and drum experience are not added. Selected testers must still join through the Google Play link they receive.
Where Google processes information
Google LLC is based in the United States. Google says that it can process information on servers worldwide. The specific storage and processing countries and onward transfers for these applications, emails, and tester lists have not been confirmed. Google’s public data center locations are for reference.
Deletion by the operator
The deadlines in section 2 cover application responses, recruitment and selection emails, downloaded lists, and tester registrations the operator manages. Emails for questions and feedback follow the app privacy policy’s provisions on contact information.
Deletion from Google systems
Deletion from Google systems and backups can take additional time. Google’s general retention guidance says deletion typically takes about two months and encrypted backups may remain for up to six months, with exceptions for technical delays, security, or legal obligations. These periods are general guidance. The 30-day deadlines apply to the operator’s deletion steps.

The Google Privacy Policy applies to IP addresses, device and usage information, and Google Account information that Google collects directly when you use its services.

Pending confirmation. The exact processing countries and applicable contractual and legal requirements for international transfers are still being confirmed. Google’s help page explains that consumer Gmail and Drive do not offer an enterprise data processing agreement. The application’s consent to collection and use is distinct from separate consent to an international transfer. We will update the notice and obtain separate consent if required.

4. Consent, withdrawal, and your rights

You can decline consent. Consent to processing the required information is needed to complete your application. You can leave the optional question blank. Email druminghyuk@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal. The operator may request the minimum identity verification needed to handle your request.

You can choose whether to submit information through Google. Applying and registering for this recruitment use the Google services listed above. After applying, you can request withdrawal and deletion of recruitment information stored in those services. The operator removes unnecessary information and tester registrations. Removing a registration may limit your ability to install or update the closed test. Email copies held by recipients and records maintained by Google are handled separately.

5. Eligibility and protection

This recruitment is open to people aged 14 or older. We ask you to confirm your eligibility. If we identify an application from someone under 14, we delete it.

We use responses and lists for test administration and restrict access to what that work requires. We do not publish applicants’ information on the website or on GitHub. You will not be asked for account passwords, verification codes, or complete audio files.

6. This notice and updates

This notice is hosted on GitHub Pages. The application form opens separately. The GitHub Privacy Statement applies to connection information processed by GitHub. If recruitment or data handling changes, we will update this notice and follow the applicable consent process.
